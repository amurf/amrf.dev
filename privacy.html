Mobile Apps Privacy Policy

For applications developed by Ashley Murphy.

This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information in connection with the mobile applications developed by Ashley Murphy (the "Apps"). We are committed to maintaining the highest level of privacy and safety, particularly for our youngest users.

This policy currently applies to the following application:

  • Alphabentical (iOS & Android)

1. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our Apps (such as Alphabentical) are educational applications designed for children of all ages, including toddlers and pre-school children. Accordingly, we comply fully with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect, store, share, or request any personally identifiable information or personal data from children under the age of 13, nor do we collect it from any other user of our Apps.

2. Zero Data Collection & Transmission

We believe that children's gameplay should be completely private. When using the App:

  • No Personal Information: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, or any other personal identifier.
  • No Device Identifiers: We do not track or collect unique device IDs (like IDFA, AAID, or UUIDs), IP addresses, or hardware details.
  • Local Gameplay: All game state, spelling progress, and levels are stored locally on your device. None of this data is transmitted to remote servers.
  • No Permissions Required: The App does not require access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, or photos.

3. No Ads or Third-Party SDKs

To ensure a safe environment free of distractions, tracking, and inappropriate content:

  • There are no advertisements of any kind within the App.
  • We do not include any third-party analytics SDKs, tracking pixels, or monetization scripts.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
